About the app

This Privacy Policy applies to the Earthfall Squadron Android game published by DariasApp (package name: com.dariasapp.earthfallsquadron).

Earthfall Squadron is a one-finger portrait arcade space shooter. It does not require an account or sign-in. DariasApp does not host a user profile on its own servers and does not sell personal data through the app.

Information we process

The app does not send your name, email, or phone number to DariasApp. Progress, settings, and language stay on the device.

Ads and Google AdMob

The app may show a Google AdMob App Open ad when it launches. Ads are not shown during gameplay or missions.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP address, advertising ID, device information, and app interactions for serving, measurement, security, and (where allowed) personalization, under Google’s policies.

In required regions, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for ad preferences. You can reopen that choice from Ad privacy in Settings.

Google Privacy Policy

Data sharing

DariasApp does not sell personal data obtained through Earthfall Squadron.

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads — app-open ads
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — ad consent and preference messages
  • Google Play services — distribution and related platform functionality

These providers may process data under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear app data. Retention of Google advertising data follows Google’s policies.

Children’s privacy

Earthfall Squadron is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Age ratings and target-audience settings are configured in Google Play Console.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the game’s behavior or services change. The “Last updated” date on this page will change when that happens.
