Darias Wordloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Türkçe · English

The app

This policy applies to the Darias Wordloom Android app published by DariasApp (package: com.dariasapp.wordloom). Wordloom is an offline English learning app that teaches everyday words and phrases from A1 to C2 with pictures, Turkish reading guides, speech, flashcards and short tests.

The app does not create an account. DariasApp does not host a user profile on its own servers and does not sell personal data through the app.

Learning data on the device

Age is not asked and is not collected. Level, completed units, review history, daily goal, language and settings stay on this device and are not sent to DariasApp. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data removes this information. You can also reset learning progress from the Account screen.

Coins and locked units

The first unit of every level is free; other units open for 3 in-app coins. 10 coins are a gift on the first install. Coins are earned only by watching an optional rewarded ad to the end (+10). Coins cannot be bought and have no cash value.

The coin balance and the units opened with coins are stored on the device and backed up with Google Block Store. If backup is on in your Google account, this backup is kept end-to-end encrypted by Google, so coins come back after an uninstall and reinstall and the gift is not given again. The backup only holds the coin count, the codes of unlocked units and whether the gift was given; DariasApp cannot access it.

Full version (in-app purchase)

The optional "Full version" is a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing; it opens every unit and removes all ads in the app. Google Play processes the payment; payment and card details never reach DariasApp. The purchase is tied to your Google account and is restored from Google Play after a reinstall. Purchase records follow Google's policies.

Ads and Google services

Users without the full version see two kinds of Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK ads: one app-open ad when the app starts (not on the first use) and rewarded ads that open only when the user asks for one. Banner and interstitial ads are not used. Ad requests are not tagged as child-directed; ad content is capped at teen-appropriate (T).

In required regions, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) collects ad privacy choices and ads are not loaded without UMP permission. Google may process IP address, device/app information, interactions and diagnostics for serving, measuring and securing ads. The Android advertising ID (AD_ID) permission is removed.

Privacy choices

Where required, UMP shows an ad privacy form, which can be reopened from the Account screen. Ad preferences can also be managed in your device's Google account settings.

Speech and reading guides

English speech uses the device text-to-speech service. A voice pack may need internet. The app does not record the microphone. Turkish reading guides ship inside the app (derived from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary); no data is sent for them.

Children

The app is an English learning app for a general audience; age is not collected. DariasApp does not collect a child's name, email or account.

Retention

DariasApp does not operate a Wordloom user account or remote user database. Local app data can be cleared in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. The Block Store backup follows your Google account backup settings. Retention of data processed by Google AdMob, UMP and Google Play follows Google's policies.
