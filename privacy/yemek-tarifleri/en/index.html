Yemek Tarifleri Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Türkçe · English

The app

This policy applies to the Yemek Tarifleri Android app published by DariasApp (package: com.dariasapp.yemektarifleri). It is a kitchen companion with an original offline recipe catalogue, ingredient matching, favorites, a shopping list, weekly planning, cooking mode and a timer.

The app does not create an account. DariasApp does not host a user profile on its own servers and does not sell personal data through the app.

Data stored on the device

Favorites, weekly plans, pantry items, shopping list, recent recipes, theme, language and timer settings stay on this device and are not sent to DariasApp. Recipe behaviour is not sent to a DariasApp server. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data removes this information.

Calorie figures shown on recipes are rough estimates. The app does not provide diet or weight tracking, and these estimates are not sent to DariasApp.

Ads and Google services

The app may show Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK banner, interstitial and app-open ads. In required regions, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages ad privacy choices. Ads are not loaded unless UMP canRequestAds is true.

These Google services may process IP address, device/app information, the Android advertising ID (AD_ID), interactions and diagnostics for serving, measuring and securing ads.

Privacy choices

Where required, UMP presents ad and privacy choices. Those choices can be opened again from inside the app (Ad privacy choices). Google ad settings can also be managed from the device’s Google/Android privacy settings.

Children’s privacy

Yemek Tarifleri is not designed for children. DariasApp does not collect names, email addresses or account details from children. Ads are shown through Google AdMob and are subject to Google’s advertising policies.

Retention and deletion

DariasApp does not operate a user account or remote user database for Yemek Tarifleri. Local app data can be cleared from Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Retention and deletion of data processed by Google AdMob and UMP follow Google’s policies and the user’s Google/Android privacy settings.
