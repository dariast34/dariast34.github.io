Last updated: 21 September 2026

Türkçe English

The app

This policy applies to the Neon Drift Android game published by DariasApp (package name: com.dariasapp.neondrift). Contact: dariast34@gmail.com.

Neon Drift is a one-finger space arcade game where you collect stars and dodge meteors. It does not require an account or sign-in.

Data we collect

The app does not ask for your name, email or phone number. Score and high score stay on the device.

Ads and Google AdMob

A Google AdMob App Open ad may be shown only the first time the app is opened. Ads are not shown on later launches or during gameplay.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP address, advertising ID, device information and app interactions for serving, measurement and (where allowed) personalization, under Google’s policies.

In required regions, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for ad preferences.

Google Privacy Policy

Children

Neon Drift is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Retention

Local scores are deleted when you uninstall the app. Retention of Google advertising data follows Google’s policies.
