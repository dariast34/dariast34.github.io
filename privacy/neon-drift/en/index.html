About the app

This Privacy Policy applies to the Neon Drift Android game published by DariasApp (package name: com.dariasapp.neondrift).

Neon Drift is a one-finger space arcade game where you collect stars and dodge meteors. It does not require an account or sign-in. DariasApp does not host a user profile on its own servers and does not sell personal data through the app.

Information we process

The app does not send your name, email, or phone number to DariasApp. Score and high score stay on the device.

Ads and Google AdMob

A Google AdMob App Open ad may be shown only the first time the app is opened. Ads are not shown on later launches or during gameplay.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP address, advertising ID, device or other identifiers, and app interactions for serving, measurement, security, and (where allowed) personalization, under Google’s policies. This data is collected and shared with Google for advertising. Collection is required to show the ad and is not limited to short-term processing in memory.

In required regions, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for ad preferences.

Google Privacy Policy

Data sharing

DariasApp does not sell personal data obtained through Neon Drift.

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads — first-open ad
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — ad consent and preference messages
  • Google Play services — distribution and related platform functionality

These providers may process data under their own privacy policies. Data is sent over an encrypted connection.

Retention and deletion

Local scores are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear app data. Retention of Google advertising data follows Google’s policies. There is no separate account-deletion flow because the app does not create accounts.

Children’s privacy

Neon Drift is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The target audience is ages 13 and over.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the game’s behavior or services change. The “Last updated” date on this page will change when that happens.
