Türkçe English

The app

This policy applies to the Earthfall Squadron Android game published by DariasApp (package name: com.dariasapp.earthfallsquadron). Contact: dariast34@gmail.com.

Earthfall Squadron is a one-finger 2D space shooter. It does not require an account or sign-in.

Accounts and data we collect

The app does not create accounts. DariasApp does not send your name, email or phone number to our servers. Progress, settings and language stay on the device.

Ads and Google AdMob

The app may show a Google AdMob App Open ad when it launches. Ads are not shown during gameplay. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP address, advertising ID, device information and app interactions for serving, measurement and (where allowed) personalization, under Google’s policies.

In required regions, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for ad preferences. You can reopen that choice from Ad privacy in Settings.

Google Privacy Policy

Children

Earthfall Squadron is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings are deleted when you uninstall the app. Retention of Google advertising data follows Google’s policies.
