Circadia Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Circadia (com.halimyazilim.circadia).

Data we process

Circadia does not require an account. Screen filter and brightness adjustments are processed on-device. If you grant location permission, approximate location may be used for sunrise/sunset timing; otherwise Istanbul (Turkey) times are used by default.

Advertising

The app may show Google AdMob ads. Google may process advertising ID and technical data under Google policies.

Permissions

  • Draw over other apps (screen filter)
  • Modify system settings (brightness)
  • Location (optional, solar times)
  • Notifications (foreground service)
