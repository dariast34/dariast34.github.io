PHONE DUEL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the PHONE DUEL Android app published by DariasApp (package name: com.dariasapp.phoneduel).

PHONE DUEL is a local two-player casual/party game. It does not provide banking, payments, crypto, or other financial features, and it is not a health app. The app does not require a user account or sign-in.

Information We Collect

PHONE DUEL does not ask you to create an account and does not request a username, password, email address, bank details, or similar personal account information for gameplay.

The app may store basic on-device settings needed to run the game, such as language or local game preferences. DariasApp does not operate a PHONE DUEL account system that stores your profile on DariasApp servers.

Because the app includes Google AdMob and related Google Play services, third-party advertising and platform services may process technical information on their own systems. That processing is described in the sections below.

Advertising

PHONE DUEL displays ads. Advertising is provided through Google AdMob and related Google services. Ads may be shown during or around gameplay.

Ad content, delivery, measurement, and any personalization are handled by those third-party services according to their own policies and, where required, applicable consent or ad settings on the device.

Google AdMob

PHONE DUEL uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) to show advertisements. Google may collect and process information to serve, measure, and improve ads, and to prevent fraud or abuse, in line with Google’s policies.

DariasApp does not control Google’s independent data processing. For details about how Google uses information in advertising products, see Google’s own privacy policy and advertising documentation.

Device and Advertising Data

Google AdMob and related Google Play services may process the following types of information according to their own policies:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • App interaction data
  • Ad performance and diagnostic information

This list describes categories that advertising and mobile platform services commonly process. DariasApp does not claim that every category is always collected in every region or on every device, and it does not claim that no other technical data can be processed by those services.

How Information Is Used

On-device settings are used to operate PHONE DUEL on your device.

Information processed by Google AdMob and related services may be used to display ads, measure ad performance, maintain and diagnose advertising features, and comply with applicable advertising and platform requirements.

DariasApp does not sell personal data collected through PHONE DUEL and does not use the app to provide financial or health services.

Third-Party Services

PHONE DUEL uses third-party services, including:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads — advertising
  • Google Play services — app distribution and related platform functionality that may support ads and device services

These providers may collect or process data under their own privacy policies. If additional third-party SDKs are added later, this policy will be updated.

Data Retention

Local game settings stored on your device remain there until you change them, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app.

Information processed by Google AdMob and other third-party services is retained according to those providers’ policies. DariasApp does not control those third-party retention periods.

Children's Privacy

PHONE DUEL is a general-audience casual/party game. It is not presented here as an app designed specifically for children.

Age ratings and target-audience settings are configured in Google Play Console and may be updated over time. DariasApp does not use PHONE DUEL to ask children for personal account information such as a name, email address, or password.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app or related third-party services, you may contact us using the details below.

Security

PHONE DUEL is a local game on your device. The security of on-device settings also depends on the security of that device.

Advertising and platform data may be transmitted through Google’s infrastructure. The security of those transfers depends on Google’s practices. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if PHONE DUEL, its advertising setup, or applicable requirements change. When it is updated, the “Last updated” date on this page will change. The current version will remain available at this URL.
