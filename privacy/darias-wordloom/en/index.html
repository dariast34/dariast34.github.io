Darias Wordloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Türkçe · English

The app

This policy applies to the Darias Wordloom Android app published by DariasApp (package: com.dariasapp.wordloom). Wordloom is an offline English vocabulary app with flashcards, short games and daily review.

The app does not create an account. DariasApp does not host a user profile on its own servers and does not sell personal data through the app.

Learning data on the device

Learning history, age range, language, favourites, daily goal and settings stay on this device and are not sent to DariasApp. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data removes this information. You can also reset learning history from Profile.

Ads and Google services

No ad requests are made for under-18 or unspecified-age profiles. Every lesson remains available without ads.

Only profiles that explicitly choose 18+ may show a Google AdMob banner. In required regions, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) collects ad privacy choices. Ads are not loaded unless UMP canRequestAds is true.

Google may process IP address, device/app information, interactions and diagnostics for serving, measuring and securing ads. The Android advertising ID (AD_ID) permission is removed in this version.

Privacy choices

Where required, UMP shows an ad privacy form. Adult profiles can reopen it from Profile → Ad preferences.

Speech

English speech uses the device text-to-speech service. A voice pack may need internet. The app does not record the microphone.

Children

The app includes learning paths for children, teens and adults. Ads are not requested for under-18 or unspecified-age profiles. DariasApp does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Retention

DariasApp does not operate a Wordloom user account or remote user database. Local app data can be cleared in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Retention of Google advertising data follows Google’s policies.
